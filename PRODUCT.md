## Stack

Owner's starting position **(confirmed)**: WordPress, because the domain harudot.com is already with SiteGround; open to an alternative if it keeps cost low.

Recommended, awaiting the owner's go-ahead: WordPress on the existing SiteGround plan, with a small custom theme written for this site (no page-builder plugin, no paid theme), free plugins only. Reason: staff who are not developers change menus and promotions often, in two languages, and need an admin screen; SiteGround already includes WordPress hosting, backups, SSL and caching, so the added cost is zero.

Open: which SiteGround plan the account is on, and whether harudot.com has hosting attached or only the domain registration. On 2026-11-26 the domain did not resolve at all (no DNS record), so nothing is live there today.

## Users

People deciding whether and how to visit a Harudot café **(confirmed by the stated objective)**: they want to see the place, find the branch, and check the menu and current promotions. Thai and English readers **(confirmed)**.

Secondary: people enquiring about private events, photo shoots, press and collaborations **(confirmed)**.

Open: the split between local regulars and travelling visitors, and what device they mostly use (assumed phone-first until told otherwise).

## Product Purpose

The first website for Harudot by Nana Coffee Roasters **(confirmed)**. Its main objective, in the owner's words: "a website with our coffee shop information and location with a lot of beautiful architectural photos", plus a menu and promotion page for each branch and a contact page.

Success, as read from the answers: a visitor sees what a branch looks like, then gets directions to it; an event or press enquiry reaches the team.

Not in scope **(confirmed by omission when offered)**: delivery-app links, and LINE / social chat as a primary action. No online shop.

## Product Principles

1. **The building is the content.** Photographs of the real places lead; words and interface step back.
2. **Every branch is findable in one step.** Whatever a visitor is looking at, the way to that branch's hours and directions is one tap away.
3. **Staff can keep it true.** Menus, promotions and hours are changed by the team without a developer, in both languages; a page with stale information is worse than a plain one.
4. **Costs nothing extra to run.** Each feature has to work on the hosting already paid for.
5. **Only real material.** Real photos with credit, real addresses, real offers; a marked gap rather than a stand-in.
