## Stack

WordPress, hosted on WordPress.com's Personal plan **(confirmed 2026-11-26)**. The domain harudot.com stays registered at SiteGround and is connected to the WordPress.com site by changing its name servers. A small theme written for this site is held in this repository; free plugins only.

Checked on WordPress.com's own pages on 2026-11-26: the Personal plan includes connecting a domain registered elsewhere, plugin installation, theme upload, SFTP and GitHub deployments, with 6 GB of storage. It does not include a staging site, one-click restore or Google Analytics. Re-check these before relying on them later; plan contents change.

On 2026-11-26 harudot.com had no DNS record, so nothing is live there.

## Users

People deciding whether and how to visit a Harudot café **(confirmed by the stated objective)**: they want to see the place, find the branch, and check the menu and current promotions. Thai and English readers **(confirmed)**.

Secondary: people enquiring about private events, photo shoots, press and collaborations **(confirmed)**.

Open: the split between local regulars and travelling visitors, and what device they mostly use (assumed phone-first until told otherwise).

## Product Purpose

The first website for Harudot by Nana Coffee Roasters **(confirmed)**. Its main objective, in the owner's words: "a website with our coffee shop information and location with a lot of beautiful architectural photos", plus a menu and promotion page for each branch and a contact page.

Success, as read from the answers: a visitor sees what a branch looks like, then gets directions to it; an event or press enquiry reaches the team.

Not in scope **(confirmed by omission when offered)**: delivery-app links, and LINE / social chat as a primary action. No online shop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two languages, Thai and English, on every page **(confirmed)**.
- Per branch: architectural photos, information and location, a menu, current promotions **(confirmed)**.
- Visitor actions: get directions (Google Maps, hours, phone) and enquire about events, press and collaborations through a contact form **(confirmed)**.
- Visitors pick a branch from a map of Thailand **(confirmed)**. All seven branches lie within about 150 km of Bangkok, so the map needs an enlarged area around Bangkok, Chonburi and Khao Yai to tell them apart.
- Approved additions **(confirmed)**: "open now" from entered hours, start and end dates on promotions, a "coming soon" branch state, a few signature items typed as text beside the menu images, branch details marked up for search engines, and a press and awards page later.
- Low running cost is a hard constraint **(confirmed)**: nothing that adds a monthly fee without the owner's agreement.
- Professional photo sets exist for some branches only **(confirmed)**; the rest have casual or social-media photos for now. The site must look complete when a branch has few photos.

Open: which branches have professional sets; where enquiries should be delivered (email address); whether a link to Nana Coffee Roasters for buying beans is wanted.

## Product Principles

1. **The building is the content.** Photographs of the real places lead; words and interface step back.
2. **Every branch is findable in one step.** Whatever a visitor is looking at, the way to that branch's hours and directions is one tap away.
3. **Staff can keep it true.** Menus, promotions and hours are changed by the team without a developer, in both languages; a page with stale information is worse than a plain one.
4. **Costs nothing extra to run.** Each feature has to work on the hosting already paid for.
5. **Only real material.** Real photos with credit, real addresses, real offers; a marked gap rather than a stand-in.
