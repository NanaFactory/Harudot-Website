# Surface brief: Harudot home and branch sheet (design prototype)

Scope: `prototype/index.html` (home) and `prototype/chonburi.html` (branch sheet). Visitor mode: Experience. Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype for the owner's review; it becomes the WordPress theme on WordPress.com afterwards.

Audience and job: someone on a phone who has seen a Harudot building and wants to see it properly, pick a branch, check hours, and get directions. Secondary: event, press and collaboration enquiries.

Content on hand: 86 professional photographs of Chonburi only; logo; the haruno display face; address, phone and one day's hours for all seven branches from Google Maps; 26 drink photographs. No photographs for the other six branches, no menu images, no promotions.

Constraints: Thai and English with equal weight; photographs shown whole; staff-maintained content; nothing that needs a paid service.

Unresolved: full weekly hours; photographs for six branches; web licence for haruno (copyright IDIN Architects); photographer credit wording; Thai brand spelling.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the drawing set of the cafés. Each branch is a sheet; a photograph is a plate on that sheet; you cross from outside to inside by dragging a section cut. It refuses the café-site default of a mood hero, a story block and a card grid of locations.

OWN-WORLD: Cool drafting-film white ground, black line in two weights (fine for everything drawn and ruled, heavy for the ground line and section heads) plus the one dashed cut line; one fill only: clay pink used the way a section fills what it cuts (hatch under the ground line, the selected branch, the primary action). Ruled title blocks hold facts. Section marks, leader lines, scale bar, north arrow, crossed-rectangle for a plate not yet supplied. Lettering: haruno, the brand's own monoline face, for names; Bai Jamjuree for Thai and English text, in its light weight wherever it stands beside haruno (Thai names, numerals). No shadows, gradients, rounded cards or glass; depth by overlap only.

STORY: The visitor sees the building, understands there are seven and where they are, picks one from the map, sees whether it is open, and taps Directions.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, about 60% of the width: the plate of the selected branch, outside and inside joined at a dashed cut line with A marks, draggable. Right: the detail plan with seven points, named leaders and a key plan of Thailand. Under both: the heavy ground line with clay hatch, then the title block of the selected branch with hours, phone, Directions (primary, clay) and Open sheet. On a phone: plate, ground line, title block, map, list. (Changed during the build: the title block moved ahead of the map so that Directions sits inside the first viewport.)

FORM: architect's drawing set (site plan and section drawing), position 5 on the ordered list; seed:485ecc44a2594ed7. Signature interaction: the section cut. Motion grammar: lines draw (the map's leaders, once, on load), plates wipe along the cut; nothing fades up or floats. Controls are worded for a café visitor, not in drawing-office terms.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
